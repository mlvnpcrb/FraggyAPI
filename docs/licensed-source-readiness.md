## Evidence required before a real pilot

Obtain separately authorized dataset use from the user. Keep the dataset, private
manifest, agreement and operator report in ignored/private storage. Establish the
supplier's authority, exact dataset/version/hash, lawful collection method,
storage permission, retained/derived field list, commercial redistribution scope,
required credits, modification disclosure, license compatibility, expiry and
withdrawal procedures. Review current terms and relevant robots directives if any
later collection is proposed; neither grants commercial redistribution by itself.
Record evidence references, reviewer, date and unresolved questions privately.

An operator must explicitly approve the manifest digest and supported obligations.
Unknown or unsupported conditions mean storage-only review or no authorization,
never automatic approval. Record and field publication remain separate decisions.
No authorization event enables an adapter. The seven website IDs are reserved and
cannot be approved through the file-import CLI. A genuinely authorized file from
an independent provider needs its own reviewed source identity and evidence.
